## Generierungsprompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square app icon artwork for Abspann, an independent personal movie watchlist and mood-based film picker. Full bleed opaque dark forest green background #151a17, no rounded outer corners (operating system supplies the mask), no surrounding mockup. Centered single memorable warm pale-lime #d3ed9b emblem: an elegant bold film ribbon forming a lowercase a, with a simple triangular play aperture in its negative space and just a few generous film perforations. Sophisticated indie cinema identity, flat vector-like precision, very subtle dimensional tonal contrast only, strong silhouette readable at 32px. Emblem occupies central 65% of canvas with generous safe margins. No words, no typography, no additional ornaments, no gradients that look metallic, no glow, no drop shadow, no border. Calm premium editorial design, not a generic clapperboard. 1024x1024 square.
